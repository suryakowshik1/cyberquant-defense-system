## 🎯 2. Problem Statement Abstract
Enterprises invest millions in disparate cybersecurity tools, vulnerability scanners, and compliance frameworks. Yet cyber risk continues to be communicated through subjective qualitative labels such as "Low", "Medium", or "High". 

These vague labels fail to express financial exposure in boardroom terms (₹ INR / $ USD), leaving CISOs, CFOs, and Board of Directors unable to answer three fundamental questions:
1. *What is our actual monetary risk exposure if a major breach occurs?*
2. *Which vulnerabilities should be prioritized for remediation first?*
3. *How do we mathematically allocate our limited security budget to achieve maximum risk reduction?*

---

## 💡 3. The CyberQuant Solution
CyberQuant bridges technical telemetry and executive capital allocation through:
1. **Interactive 3D SOC Command Center:** Three.js tactical globe visualizing 31 global hub facilities, live breach telemetry, CVSS scores, and threat propagation vectors.
2. **Open FAIR™ Actuarial Risk Engine:** Calculates Single Loss Expectancy (SLE), Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO), and Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE in ₹ Lakhs & Crores).
3. **0/1 Knapsack Budget Optimization:** Mathematical solver finding the exact portfolio of security mitigations that maximizes avoided loss within budget, computing Return on Security Investment (ROSI).
4. **Passive Perimeter Website Scanner:** Non-invasive scanner testing SSL/TLS, HTTP security headers (CSP, HSTS), and exposed attack surfaces.
5. **Zero-Trust Read-Only Database Auditor:** Audits PostgreSQL, MySQL, MSSQL, MongoDB, and SQLite instances to calculate DPDP Act (2023) liability without viewing private customer records.
